About us

Built By An Operator Who Hit The Ceiling Himself

Mathew (Matej Serbin) started in e-commerce as a teenager in Slovakia, reselling AirPods and then selling through TikTok Shop. In the spring of 2025 one of his organic dropshipping products went viral and his store did close to $600,000 in a single month.

He lost almost all of it within the year. A payment processor held his funds, the business behind the store was never built to carry that volume, and he made decisions as the person he was before the money arrived. He rebuilt from scratch on paid ads in October 2025, and today his brand runs on a full team, its own software and six figures a month in revenue.

That experience is why Ascend Mastery exists. Most e-commerce education teaches products, ads and suppliers, and the founders who come to us usually know those already. What holds them at $20,000 to $50,000 a month is the way they think about money, risk and themselves, so that is the part we teach.

Our mission is to make 100 millionaires through Ascend Mastery.

What we do

What Ascend Mastery Includes

Ascend Mastery is delivered online and members keep lifetime access to the course material. We don't sell physical products and we don't run stores on anyone's behalf.

01

The Identity Course

Our flagship module, "How To Transcend Your Identity", is a set of short video lessons with exercises that walk a founder through reading, rewriting and holding the identity their business runs on.

02

Ongoing Tracks

Two growing lesson tracks, Expanding Your Awareness and Removing Your Limiting Beliefs, which add new lessons over time.

03

The Identity Toolbox

A 108-page action book with the tools, environments and 30 and 90-day reviews members use between lessons, plus a guided AI self-audit.
